a CUDA-corespMspV kernel for decode and a Tensor-Core
SpMM kernel for prefill, allowing a single compressed format to sup-
port both phases of LLM inference. Our evaluations show thatSPDP
improves kernel throughput, TPOT, and power efficiency over prior
sparse baselines while maintaining model quality. Future work in-
cludes extendingSPDPto batched dynamic pruning, production
serving integration, and multi-GPU sparsity-aware scheduling.
