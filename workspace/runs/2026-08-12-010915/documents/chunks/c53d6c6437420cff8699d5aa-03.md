5 Conclusion
This paper introducedDeltaServe, a host-agnostic co-serving
design that folds LoRA fine-tuning into an existing inference
engine under SLO control, driven by a CUDA-graph-aware
latency model and realized on vLLM, SGLang, and S-LoRA.
On a Nutanix production trace it delivers2.9× the fine-tuning
throughput of LLMStation at100%inference SLO compli-
ance versus LLMStation’s85%, harvesting idle GPU capacity
without compromising interactive latency.
