Synthesis construction.The synthesis planner takes all five Base
repositories in each language group and chooses one Base snapshot

three file hint, three module hint, three traversal, two reasoning, and
two symbol hint queries. Behavioral anchors are non-test source

one-hop neighbors provide disambiguating context. Traversal rows
