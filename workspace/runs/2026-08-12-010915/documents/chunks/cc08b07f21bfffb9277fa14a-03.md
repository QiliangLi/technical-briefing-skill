B MCore and Hugging Face Parameter Layouts
This appendix explains why AREAL-DTE performs change detection after conversion rather than directly in
the optimizer-native MCore layout. A fused QKV linear layer provides a concrete example; the same principle
applies whenever training and inference layouts differ because of sharding, fusion, permutation, or naming.
B.1 Optimizer-Native MCore Layout
MCore organizes model parameters for distributed training. For a tensor-parallel (TP) linear layer, each TP
rank stores a shard of the logical weight matrix, and several logical projections may be fused into one physical
parameter. During AdamW optimization, every locally owned FP32 master-weight slice is element-wise aligned
with its first- and second-moment states. This alignment makes the optimizer-native MCore layout the natural
space for the reconstruction in Section 4.1.
However, a local MCore index identifies an element of a physical training tensor rather than necessarily
one logical inference parameter. Such an index cannot be applied directly by an inference worker without
accounting for fusion, permutation, and sharding.

logical inference weight appears as a separately named tensor with a fixed element order. For the QKV layer
considered below, the canonical tensors are
