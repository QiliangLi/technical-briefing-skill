VII. RELATEDWORK
Tree Speculative Decoding.Speculative decoding accelerates
autoregressive inference by verifying a sequence of drafted
tokens in parallel [5], [8], [16], [17], [22], [41], [51]. Among
these methods, tree speculation stands out for longer accepted
paths and greater speedups [4], [6], [19], [20]. Medusa [4]
and EAGLE-3 [20] generate branches with learned predictors,
while Sequoia [6], EAGLE-2 [19], Yggdrasil [9], and GTO [11]
optimize static or probability-aware tree topologies. SpecIn-
fer [26] and DeFT [47] reduce target-side verification cost.
These works do not transfer to recurrent linear attention, which
exposes no attention matrix [26], [47]. AdaServe [21] allocates
proposal budgets across requests to meet SLO requirements.
Hybrid-Attention Model Serving.vLLM [15], SGLang [53],
and related systems optimize batching, caching, and GPU

## PDF Page 12

execution [1], [13], [32], [33], [48], [54]. For hybrid models,
Marconi [31] caches recurrent prefixes, while HLX [12] and
Pimba [14] specialize Transformer–SSM execution. STree [44]
composes diagonal SSM transitions over proposal trees, but
this algebra does not extend to the token-dependent, non-
diagonal recurrence of gated linear attention. These systems
do not jointly support parallel gated-linear tree verification
and transient branch-state management [39], [44], [46]. Bole
provides both within a production engine.
VIII. CONCLUSION
We present Bole, a kernel–runtime co-design for efficient
tree speculative decoding of hybrid-attention LLMs. Its closed-
form solver parallelizes recurrent tree verification, while
factorized state management commits only the accepted branch.
A hardware-aware batch-wide budget turns these gains into
serving throughput. Integrated into SGLang, Bole accelerates
linear-attention verification by up to 7.7×, reduces transient
state memory by 82–99×, and outperforms the strongest tree-
serving baseline by up to 2.03×. On online agent workloads,
it reduces TTFT and TPOT by up to 67.6% and 49.9%,
respectively, over the strongest tree-speculative baseline.
