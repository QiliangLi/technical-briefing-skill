## Available summary

Dual-granularity context management framework (Ingestion-Aware Compaction + Lifecycle-Aware Eviction) that preserves prompt-cache continuity for long agent sessions, reducing inference cost 56-87% on PinchBench and Claw-Eval; integrated into LightMem2 with open-source code.
