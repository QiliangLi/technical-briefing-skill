B.3 Cache behavior
Provider-side prefix caching is automatic and free to populate in the original serving route. It
rebates approximately 61% of the would-be bill (billed cost approximately 39% of no-cache cost)
while leaving behavioral metrics unchanged. Hits are probabilistic: full, partial, and no hit can
alternate under identical conditions. The cache is shared across sessions; fresh Claude Code sessions
routinely receive first-turn hits covering roughly 90% of the 16–20k prefix. A 60-second delay showed
no eviction, while changing the working directory breaks reuse because the directory is embedded in
the system prompt. Nemotron through PI.DEV received zero cached tokens throughout all phases
for three days, a provider-side anomaly that would distort comparisons based only on billed cost.
The first-party claude-sonnet-5 study received cache rebates of 69–75%. Because caching changes
price without changing agent behavior, the paper keeps behavioral work, actual billed cost, and
no-cache cost conceptually separate.
B.4 Native Claude Code billing reconciliation and classifiers
The 5–30× open-model harness gap cannot be caused by Anthropic-side classifiers because those
requests were served by Together AI through the translation gateway and never entered Anthropic’s
model-serving path. The measured gap is accounted for by the larger prefix, 2–7 × more turns, and
verification-heavy tool composition.
For the 54 native Claude Code runs against claude-sonnet-5, recorded cost reconciles from visible
usage categories: uncached input, cache reads, cache writes, and output. The median discrepancy is
zero and 53/54 runs reconcile within 1%. An initial uniform 33.3% discrepancy came from comparing
standard with introductory pricing. Remaining differences imply cache-write rates whose median
and 90th percentile equal the documented five-minute write premium (1.25 × input rate), with a
small upper tail consistent with one-hour-TTL writes (2 × input rate) mixed within a run. The
residual tracks cache-write token volume rather than request count or turns. No separately billed
classifier cost is detectable. This is a claim about billing, not about whether provider-side classifiers
execute or add latency.
B.5 Harness tool composition
At baseline, 52% of Claude Code tool calls are test executions versus 22% under PI.DEV. PI.DEV
is inspection-dominated, with 40% reads versus 21% under Claude Code. The original open models
average 14.48 calls per baseline run under Claude Code and 6.30 under PI.DEV; claude-sonnet-5
averages 5.67 under native Claude Code and 3.46 under PI.DEV. Extreme runs with 77–79 failed
calls are Claude Code+GLM permission-friction loops distributed across prompt conditions, and
are classified as harness-caused rather than prompt-caused.

results, assistant output, and harness scaffolding. Dollars, latency, and call counts are therefore
reported separately; no direct tool dollar cost is fabricated.

D.2 Condition-blind semantic judge
A frozen rubric defines 14 composition counts, six waste mechanisms, and ordinal quality scores
(H-S1–H-S6). The judge never sees prompt text, variant names, or condition labels. Grammar-
constrained JSON and mandatory evidence quotations yield 98.9% valid annotations; 91.1% of

Same-model prompt-sensitivity agreement is κ = 0.44; cross-judge-model agreement is κ = 0.28
overall and 0.55–0.68 on the hypothesis-critical fields: alternatives, unused branches, and redundant
verification. Judge-sensitive fields such as task restatement and post-solution reasoning rely on
deterministic proxies in the quantitative claims. Appendix G provides verbatim evidence examples.
D.3 Tool taxonomy and completion proxy
A second frozen rubric classifies navigation, search, read, edit, test, build, lint, git, environment,
and recovery calls. Redundancy rules are conservative: duplicate normalized commands with no
intervening edit, re-reads of unchanged files, and reruns of already-green test scopes. Unit tests
freeze the extractor; blind hand-derived labels caught one TAP pass-detection bug, which was fixed
before condition-aware analysis.
“Post-success” uses a recoverable visible-test proxy: the last edit and first fully green visible
test. The moment at which hidden evaluators would have passed during a run is not observable.
PI.DEV also lacks per-call timing, and local CPU, GPU, and memory telemetry were not recorded.
D.4 Distributional and causal limits
Tool counts are zero-inflated and heavy-tailed: 94% of runs have zero post-green repeat tests, with a
maximum of six. H-T1 and H-T4 tool-layer effects are directional rather than paper-robust. Prompt
variants are assigned within paired experimental blocks, but semantic mechanism levels are observed
after treatment; mechanism-level cost curves therefore do not identify randomized mediator effects.
Cost uses pinned catalog prices at collection time. Provider-side safety classifiers may contribute
latency, but server-side classification, queueing, and generation time cannot be separated from
exposed usage fields; absence of unexplained billing is not evidence that no classifier runs.

E Post-Registration Replications and Expansion
E.1 Kimi-K3 replication
Within a day of Kimi-K3 appearing on the provider, the authors froze a protocol covering six
pilot tasks, four variants (baseline, multiple approaches, deep thinking, bounded efficiency),
72 PI.DEV runs, and a 24-run Claude Code directional arm. Material difference was defined
before results as a reasoning ratio differing by more than 30% from both Kimi-K2 comparators, a
classification flip, or more than five percentage points of scope-compliant-success change.
The PI.DEV arm completed 72/72 at 100% success, with an 81% cache rebate. Kimi-K3’s
baseline median is 55 reasoning tokens versus 351 and 195 for the K2 comparators. Multiple
approaches reaches 16.6× [10.7, 34.1] and deep thinking 14.8 × [4.1, 33.9] against that low baseline.
Absolute multiple- approaches reasoning remains at or below K2-generation levels (625 versus 1,606
median), and baseline cost per compliant success is the lowest of the three generations despite
2.5–3.3× unit prices. Bounded efficiency is 0.89 × with its confidence interval below 1. Effect
directions and classifications are preserved.
E.2 First-party claude-sonnet-5 study
The first-party study reverses the original stack: both harnesses call the Anthropic API, and Claude
Code runs natively without gateway translation or model aliasing. The frozen protocol contains
nine variants, six pilot tasks, and two repetitions: 108 PI.DEV runs and 54 native-Claude Code
runs. All 162 are valid.
The Anthropic API includes thinking in output tokens without reporting it separately. The
preregistered primary metric is therefore total-output-token ratio, defined consistently on the open-
model side. Multiple approaches is 2.70 × [2.14, 3.86] under PI.DEV and 2.52 × [1.73, 3.48] under
native Claude Code. Verbose repetition, autonomy, and bounded efficiency remain approximately
neutral (0.93–1.02× for bounded efficiency). Native-Claude Code baseline no-cache cost per compliant
success is approximately 15 × PI.DEV’s. Maximum certainty reaches 4.13 × output [1.42, 8.57] and
2.7×no-cache cost per success. Deep thinking is 1.25–1.30×. Scope compliance is 162/162.
E.3 Registered expansion
The expansion targets four evidence gaps: (a) paraphrase generalization through 25 frozen arms
including four forms per condition, length-matched controls, and instruction-position variants; (b)
repetition stability; (c) 24 new tasks including a long-validation family and a second frozen 16-task
holdout; and (d) per-model precision. Planned scale is approximately 5,600 new valid runs and
approximately 10,250 total. Failed replications are committed to prominent reporting.
At writing, the paraphrase and stability arms contain 2,105 valid runs of 2,106: three models
× six tasks × 25 arms × three new repetitions, and two models × 16 tasks × nine variants
× three new repetitions. The registered stability analysis reports within-cell no-cache cost CV
over five-repetition cells. All four paraphrase forms of multiple approaches, deep thinking, and
maximum certainty elevate reasoning on every model tested (12/12 condition–model cells, four
of four forms). Length-matched controls are 0.96–1.05 ×. All five bounded- efficiency forms are
0.85–0.93×. Multiple-approaches magnitude ranges from 3.0 × for the concise form to 6.7 × for the
verbose form; start versus end placement shifts an example effect from 5.2 × to 4.3×. Within-cell
no-cache cost CV is 12–23%, and success is unanimous in 81–97% of cells. No effect-direction reversal
is observed for a confirmed waste condition; reversal rate 0.33 appears only for verbose repetition
