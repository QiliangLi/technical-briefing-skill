As described in Sections 4.3 and 4.4, both the ANERcorp and ARCD fine-tuning results use a two-stage warm-start
recipe: the pretrained MLM checkpoint is first prefinetuned on a task-adjacent silver- or larger-scale dataset, and the
resulting checkpoint is then fine-tuned on the small gold set itself. Table 9 reports the hyperparameters used for both
prefinetuning stages.
