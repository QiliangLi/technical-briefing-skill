#### 4.2.2. Network planning and design for training based on ACK LINGJUN

Alibaba Cloud Container Service for Kubernetes (ACK) LINGJUN managed cluster is a cluster type provided by ACK for Intelligent Computing LINGJUN. It offers a fully managed and high-availability control plane for a standard Kubernetes cluster—supporting the use of Lingjun compute nodes as worker nodes. Through deep management of infrastructure resources, ACK LINGJUN Clusters implement GPU direct connection, NUMA binding, multi-NIC multi-queue optimization, and custom scheduling policies—fully unlocking the performance potential of ultra-large-scale GPU clusters.

Network overview

- Pod network, part of the container network, connects to the VPC through the Terway network plugin. It connects to the user’s other private networks and the Internet through the VPC and TR—such as VPCs in the same or different regions on the cloud, or on-premises IDCs.

Pod network, part of the container network, connects to the VPC through the Terway network plugin. It connects to the user’s other private networks and the Internet through the VPC and TR—such as VPCs in the same or different regions on the cloud, or on-premises IDCs.

- Service network, part of the container network, is implemented by the native Kubernetes service mechanism. It provides service discovery and load balancing capabilities within the cluster based on kube-proxy and iptables/IPVS rules. In an ACK LINGJUN cluster, the service’s ClusterIP address range is independent of the VPC CIDR block.
