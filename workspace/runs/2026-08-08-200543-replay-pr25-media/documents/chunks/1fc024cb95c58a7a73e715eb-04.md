We evaluate how the selection budgetKaf-

2WikiMultiHopQA. Table 16 reports exact match

use the 1B full checkpoint.
The two datasets show different behavior. On
HotpotQA-Distractor, increasingKdoes not im-
prove answer quality: performance is best atK=
2, remains similar atK= 4, and then gradually
decreases. This suggests that larger selection bud-

text into the reconstructed memory. On 2Wiki-
MultiHopQA, score-based selection improves as

broader selected memory set, while HotpotQA-

context.
