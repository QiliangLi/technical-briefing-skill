# TurboRetry: Mitigating Large-Scale QUIC Handshake Floods with Off-the-Shelf DPU Offloading

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02264

## Evidence locator: Available summary

## Available summary

TurboRetry 将 QUIC Retry 机制拆分并卸载到 DPU，同时在 on-path DPA 上执行连接授权；扩展 Retry token 维持协议语义并减少主机协同开销，吞吐比主机实现提高 10–20 倍。
