# Topology-Aware Data Movement for Disaggregated GPU Inference

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28633

## Evidence locator: Available summary

## Available summary

Argues that uniform RDMA for inter-pool KV cache transfer ignores 72x bandwidth variation across GPU pairs, and proposes a topology-aware orchestrator selecting among NVLink, PCIe, RDMA, and CXL transports, reporting 3-18x transfer latency reduction over uniform RDMA.
