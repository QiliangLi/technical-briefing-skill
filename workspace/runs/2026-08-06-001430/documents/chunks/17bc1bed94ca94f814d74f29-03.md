While our review of the existing literature shows various performance analysis tools
for GPUs, support for vGPUs remains a significant gap. Unfortunately, the only tool offer-
ing vGPU support, NVIDIA Nsight Systems, is closed-source software and lacks publicly
available documentation. To address this gap in research, our study introduces a novel
diagnostic tool that is specifically designed to analyze vGPU performance. Utilizing GVT-g,
Intel’s virtualization technology for on-die GPUs, as a foundational platform, our frame-
work provides detailed insights into GVT-g operations. It facilitates an exhaustive analysis
of VMs equipped with vGPUs, thereby enhancing the understanding and optimization of
their performance.
Our approach leverages LTTng, a low-overhead tracer, to collect the low-level data re-
quired for our analyses. Through this tool, we traced the host machine kernel to gain a better
understanding of vGPU resource utilization. Furthermore, we developed a suite of auto-

such as MxGPU and NVIDIA Grid. These technologies have gained prominence in the data
