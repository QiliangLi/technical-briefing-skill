diagnosis (extreme, cross-model non-normality of A; heavy-tailed weight spectra; hub-shaped
cross-subspace coupling) explains why weight-only and invariant-subspace indexes fail, and why
data-aware reconstruction was the right direction with the wrong objective.
1 Introduction
At a million tokens of context, an 8B-parameter model’s KV cache occupies ∼130 GB in bf16 —
decoding becomes a retrieval problem. Practical systems separateselectionfromreading: a cheap
index over cached keys nominates candidates, exact values are fetched for the winners [ 11, 5], and
sparse architectures build the split into training [ 1]. The index is a rank- r sketch of the keys; its
quality gates everything downstream.
What subspace should the index use? The field’s answers fall into two objective classes.Weight-
side: truncate the projection matrices — optimal for the operator, blind to data.Data-aware
reconstruction: preserve the cached tensors under the activation distribution — from key PCA
through its increasingly sophisticated 2026 descendants [ 18, 19, 20, 21]. On a common protocol
(all 1,024 heads of LLaMA-3.1-8B, 4,096-token natural text, exact queries, top-64 recall) the class
representatives achieve 0.366 (weight-SVD) and 0.748 (key-PCA) atr=32.
Neither class optimizes what attention uses. Scores are s = q⊤k; retrieval needs theirranking.

7 Experiments
Main result.SAKI-opt beats PCA at every rank (Table 1, Fig. 1); framed against what remains,
it removes 16%, 20%, and 26% of PCA’s residual recall error at r = 16 , 32, 64. The per-head
delta at r=32 has median +0 .041 with 76 .9% of heads improving (79 .3% at r=64), and the gains
concentrate in layers 16–31 (+0 .061 median) — precisely where query and key geometry diverge
and a query-blind subspace is costliest.

The objective, not the covariances, wins.The variant ordering is exact-optimum > weighted
span > raw span ≈ PCA at every rank. Merely inserting covariance matrices into a span construction
(SAP-svd) does not beat PCA; solving the derived objective over the full class of rank- r maps does.
This is the empirical signature of the asymmetric factorization.
Theory predicts practice.The predicted-vs-actual score-MSE ablation (Remark 2, Appendix C)
shows per-head agreement at r = 0 .997 across 1,024 heads — the derivation’s independence
model describes real attention statistics almost exactly, so the closed form can be trusted as the
deployment-time optimum for its objective.

8 Extensions
Ranking-optimized refinement (primary). L is an MSE surrogate; the deployed metric is
top-k recall, and Prop. 2 says errors matter only near the margin. Initializing at the closed form
Mr and optimizing only the low-rank factors under a pairwise logistic loss on (top- k, non-top-k)
pairs around the boundary — LLM frozen — gives the two-tier storySAKI-ClosedForm(score-MSE
optimal) /SAKI-Rank(task-aligned refinement).Per-layer rank allocation: the depth trend is
strong and measured; allocation should follow it.Query-conditioned mixtures of subspaces
(router over precomputed P1 . . . PM) are promising but introduce training, routing, and baseline
burdens that would dilute this paper; we defer them.Coupling-preservation regularization: the
Schur coupling geometry (Appendix B) specifies what any index must preserve and can regularize
SAKI-Rank.

9 Limitations
Evidence is one calibration domain at 4K context, recall-only (no end-to-end generation). Cross-
model replication is done — four models, uniform wins (Table 2) — but the spread (Qwen 30%
vs. Mistral 13% of remaining error removed at r=32) is not yet explained; relating it to per-model
operator geometry is an open analysis. Calibration transfer (domain and length shift) must be
measured; OjaKV [20] explicitly targets static-subspace failure under shift, and online updates of
Σq, Σk are the natural response if SAKI proves sensitive. RoPE’s relative rotation is handled by
pre-rotation codes, not modeled in the objective (rotation-averaged covariances are the refinement);
the independence model itself is validated at r = 0.997 (Remark 2). Head-to-head comparison with
the 2026 data-aware family [ 18, 19, 20, 21] — including whether any incorporates query statistics
through the score, CARE being the closest to check — is required before submission.
10 Conclusion
Low-rank KV indexes have optimized weights or key variance; attention uses neither. Deriving
the expected score distortion yields a two-sided covariance-weighted objective whose exact rank- r
optimum is an asymmetric, training-free, closed-form score map — and the theory holds up unusually
well in practice: it predicts per-head score-MSE reduction at r = 0.997, its variant ordering appears
exactly in the measurements, and it removes a fifth to a quarter of PCA’s remaining recall error
where retrieval is hardest. The operator-geometry diagnosis explains why the field’s earlier answers
fail and what any future index must preserve.
