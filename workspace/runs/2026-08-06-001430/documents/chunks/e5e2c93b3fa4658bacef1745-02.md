Conclusion
We proposeCodeSpec, a dual-specification approach for
agentic long-horizon feature development. It constructs
evidence-grounded functional chains from feature require-
mentsandrepositoryevidence,andcompileseachchaininto
complementaryexecutablearchitectureandbehaviorspecifi-
cations. Architecture specifications preserve the integrity of
featurerealizationpaths,whilebehaviorspecificationsverify
runtimecorrectnessalongthosepaths.Together,theyprovide
fine-grained and persistent feedback throughout implemen-
tation. Experiments on FeatureBench show thatCodeSpec
consistently outperforms representative coding agents, par-
ticularly on complex and long-horizon tasks. Moreover, re-
sults on NL2Repo-Bench further demonstrate its generaliz-
ability across repository-level development settings.
