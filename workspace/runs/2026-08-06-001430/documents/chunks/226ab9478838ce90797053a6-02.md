makes the budget a free parameter, and the more tokens it is
given, the more information it preserves.

We presented ORCA, a training-free compressor that aggre-
gates 3D CT visual tokens into connected, content-adaptive
regionsandwriteseachregion’spositionbackintothetoken.
Across two datasets and five encoders it improves over Grid
average and matched token-reduction baselines, and stays
within a small margin of the uncompressed tokens at8to
